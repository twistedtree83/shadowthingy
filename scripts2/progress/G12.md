# G12 — Time scrubber: the band sweeping across the globe

Issue: #17 · Branch: `sprint/gnomon-mvp` · Status: **shipped**

## What shipped

A scrubber over the assumed time, with play/pause and a reset to the claimed
time. Every frame is a full exact solve at the unchanged grid resolution.

## The gate, and what it shows

| assumed time | cells fitting | Sydney's strength |
|---|---|---|
| 05:00 | 3,678 | 0.000 |
| 08:00 | 3,680 | 0.000 |
| 11:00 | 3,683 | 0.000 |
| **15:20 (claimed)** | **3,681** | **0.957** |
| 18:00 | 3,681 | 0.000 |
| 20:00 | 3,680 | 0.000 |

The band stays the same *size* and moves entirely across the world. For this
photograph the time assumption is completely load-bearing: get it wrong by an
hour and the location is somewhere else on Earth.

**That is the whole point of the feature.** A band that barely moves across an
hour means the assumed time is not doing much work; one that races across a
continent means it is. A reader should know which situation they are in before
quoting a location, and no static picture conveys it.

Measured: **20 solves and redraws per second**, which reads as motion.

## No caching was needed, and none was added

G10 measured a full sweep at 404 ms **in Node** and left a note saying to cache
across frames rather than coarsen the grid. In the browser the same solve takes
**17 ms** with an azimuth filter, 13 ms without, 40 ms unbounded — V8 optimises
that tight loop far better under Chromium than under `node --test`.

So the honest thing was to do nothing: every frame is an exact solve at the full
0.5° × 0.25° resolution. **The resolution is what makes the band honest, and it
would have been a poor trade to coarsen it to buy smoothness that was not
needed.** A longitude-shift approximation was considered — a change in time is
almost exactly a rotation in longitude — and rejected: it drifts by up to ~0.2°
across a day through the declination and equation of time, which is comparable
to the band width it would be approximating.

## Decisions taken

- **The scrubbed time is held separately from the claimed time.** Scrubbing is
  an exploration and must never quietly rewrite the thing being verified. The
  map caption says "assumed 08:00" so a scrubbed view cannot be mistaken for the
  claim, and the readout's claimed time is untouched.

- **Reset gives back exactly the original band**, verified to twelve decimal
  places (0.956726100867 before and after). This is trivially true precisely
  *because* nothing is cached or approximated — there is no stale state to drift
  out of agreement.

- **Play starts from the claimed time**, so the sweep reads as "and where else
  could this have been" rather than starting from an arbitrary midnight.
