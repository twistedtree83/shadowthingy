1d How the three modes coexist · two approaches Left: explicit selector (used in 1a–1c). Right: the mode falls out of what has been supplied.
A · explicit selector
VERIFY TIME LOCATE
VERIFYneeds place + time. Answers in prose.
TIMEneeds place + date. Answers on a 24-hour axis.
LOCATEneeds date only. Answers on the globe.
Predictable, and it lets a user pick a mode before supplying anything. Cost: a mode can be selected that the inputs cannot support, so the app has to explain a refusal that the user walked into.

A residual this size means one of the method's assumptions is false, and the geometry cannot tell which. In order of frequency:
01The ground under the two objects is not level — a slope of 4° would produce exactly this.
02One marked object is not vertical, or its shadow falls on a different surface.
03The image is composited from more than one source.
Shadow bearing does not depend on the vertical scale, so 118.6° ± 2.7 still stands. Verify and Time can run on azimuth alone.

The vanishing points of the marked verticals fix the focal length independently of the file's claims. A 7.9 mm gap is far outside both tolerances, which means the image has been cropped or resized since capture, or the metadata was edited. Either way it is a finding about the file, and Gnomon records it rather than resolving it.
